# Initial content verification

Reviewed on 2 October 2026. The initial set contains eleven entities, thirteen typed relationships and sixteen independently addressable claims. English, German and Spanish text is present for each entity, relationship and claim. Bibliographic titles and institution names retain their source-language form.

Dates identify the milestone described in the entry. The Cajal entry uses 1888 for the Barcelona research milestone, not his birth. The neuron-doctrine entry uses Waldeyer's 1891 synthesis. The LeNet entry describes the 1998 LeNet-5 reference, not the beginning of convolutional-network research. Backpropagation's 1986 entry describes an influential exposition and acknowledges earlier reverse-mode differentiation.

Location markers use approximate city or institutional-area coordinates. They identify research affiliations or the documented discovery location and must not be described as exact laboratory coordinates. Distributed concepts without a defensible single location have no marker. Undated institutional webpages have an explicit access-year note because the current reference schema requires a numeric year.

## Evidence and qualifications

- Golgi's 1873 method and Abbiategrasso context: [University of Pavia museum](https://museoperlastoria.unipv.it/en/camillo-golgi-2/). The Nobel educational nerve-signaling page gives a conflicting year and was not used for dating.
- Cajal's Barcelona period: [University of Barcelona faculty history](https://www.ub.edu/portal/web/medicine-health-sciences/history). His neuron-doctrine position is supported by the indexed text of his [Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/cajal-lecture.pdf).
- Waldeyer's terminology: section 8 of [Beyond the Temples of Science](https://vlp.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/essays/data/art44), Max Planck Virtual Laboratory.
- [McCulloch and Pitts, 1943](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02478259) confirms the logical abstraction and the authors' institutional affiliations. The neuron-doctrine edge is historical context, not an asserted direct influence.
- [Rosenblatt, 1958](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0042519) publication metadata and indexed article material were checked. Publisher access was unavailable. [Cornell's institutional account](https://as.cornell.edu/news/professors-perceptron-paved-way-ai-60-years-too-soon) confirms the Buffalo context. The demonstration is explicitly a modern linear-classifier simplification.
- [Hubel and Wiesel, 1962](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1359523/) is the experiment reference. [Fukushima, 1980](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00344251) explicitly names that biological inspiration and provides its Tokyo affiliation.
- [LeCun and colleagues, 1998](https://bottou.org/papers/lecun-98h) is available from a coauthor. The supplied PDF has a damaged text encoding, so content verification also relies on its readable publication record and established architecture descriptions. The map represents the AT&T affiliation and is not an exclusive claim about every author's workplace.
- [ImageNet, 2009](https://www.image-net.org/static_files/papers/imagenet_cvpr09.pdf) confirms WordNet, human annotation and the authors' Princeton affiliation. The [challenge paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0575) distinguishes the benchmark subsets from the complete database.
- [AlexNet, 2012](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/imagenet_classification_with_deep_convolutional.pdf) was consulted directly. The 15.3% result is the competition entry combining networks, not an isolated single network. The LeNet-to-ImageNet edge is a teaching transition marked as historical context.
- [Automatic differentiation survey](https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume18/17-468/17-468.pdf) supplies the earlier differentiation history. No biological equivalence is asserted for gradient backpropagation.

Some Nobel pages and PDFs returned HTTP 403 on direct retrieval; accessible search-index excerpts were used for the award records and lecture passages. These limitations are recorded in the reference metadata. The content deliberately avoids external images until their file-specific rights have been verified.

The initial prose is concise seed content for architecture validation. Longer expert essays, original-paper page locators, author-reviewed translations and additional claim granularity remain editorial expansion work.
