## Editorial distinctions

- The 1926 entry concerns sensory feedback and impulse rate, not a motor command or a universal force-to-rate conversion.
- The 1959 paper is separate from the existing 1962 Hubel–Wiesel node. A documented extension links the two.
- The 1984 date denotes the selected study, not the first observation of face-responsive neurons.
- Place and grid fields describe firing locations. A grid is not the anatomical arrangement of cells.
- Nobel years are separate from publication years and acknowledge broader research. The paper authors and laureates are not interchangeable.
- The six sequential links are labeled conceptual comparisons. They do not assert direct influence, a single anatomical pathway, or a causal chain from the retina through hippocampus and inferotemporal cortex.
